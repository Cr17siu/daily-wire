    How this brief is put together

    Every morning at 07:00 IST a scheduled job reads the public RSS feeds of 23 Indian and international outlets and keeps everything published in the last 26 hours.

    Headlines covering the same story are merged into one entry. Two headlines match when they share enough words and at least one distinctive one — a company, a figure, a named event. Words that appear across the whole morning's news, like India or crore, are treated as filler and never count as evidence on their own. The wires badge is how many outlets ran the story; each is linked so you can check.

    Stories are ranked on how many outlets carried them, how recent they are, and the weight given to the feed. The top few are capped per sector so one busy sector cannot take the whole page, and one lead is taken from each sector so the first screen is not three versions of the same story. Headlines that arrive truncated mid-sentence are dropped, and summaries that only restate their own headline are hidden.

    Nothing here is written by hand, and nothing is edited after it is built.
